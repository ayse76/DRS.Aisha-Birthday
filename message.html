✦ A SPECIAL BIRTHDAY MESSAGE ✦

Dear Aisha

Happy Birthday to one of the most special people in my life. ❤️🎂🥹

Ma garanayo xitaa eray aan ku cabbiro sida aad qaali iigu tahay, sababtoo ah xiriirka naga dhexeeya ma aha oo kaliya saaxiibtinimo. Mar waxaad ii tahay habaryar aan si weyn u ixtiraamo oo aan ku kalsoonahay, marna waxaad ii tahay saaxiibteyda ugu dhow ee aan si fudud ula wadaagi karo farxaddayda, murugadayda iyo wax kasta oo qalbigayga ku jira. 🥹❤️

Waxaan aad ugu mahadcelinayaa Alle inuu nolosheyda igu soo daray qof sidaada oo kale ah. Waxaad tahay qof aan joogitaankiisa qiimeeyo, qof aan hadalkiisa jeclahay, qof aan taladiisa qadariyo, qof mararka qaar hal eray oo ay tiraahdo igu filan yahay inaan dareemo inaanan kaligey ahayn. 🫂❤️

Maalinta dhalashadaada waxaan rabaa inaan kuu rajeeyo wax kasta oo wanaagsan. Ilaahay ha kuu kordhiyo cimriga adigoo caafimaad qaba, qalbi faraxsan iyo nolol barako leh. 🤲🏽✨ Ilaahay ha kuu furo albaab kasta oo kheyr ah, ha kuu fududeeyo riyooyinka aad qalbiga ku sidato, hana kuu beddelo dhammaan waxyaabihii ku dhibay farxad iyo deganaan.

Waxaan rajeynayaa in sanadkan cusub ee noloshaada uu noqdo sanad aad ku gaarto waxyaabo badan oo aad muddo rajeynaysay. Sanad aad ku aragto naftaada meel ka wanaagsan tii aad shalay joogtay. Sanad ay farxaddu kugu badato, dadka ku jecelna ay kugu hareereeyaan. ❤️✨

I hope you always remember how loved, appreciated and special you are. Ha iloobin inaad tahay qof mudan in la jeclaado, la qadariyo oo mar walba loo rajeeyo kheyr. 🥹❤️

Thank you for being my habaryar, my friend, my safe person and someone I can always appreciate having in my life. Waxaan rajeynayaa in xiriirkeennu sida uu maanta u qurux badan yahay uu weligiis sii ahaado. 🫂❤️

May Allah protect you from every sadness, bless your heart, give you endless happiness, and make every coming year of your life more beautiful than the one before it. 🤲🏽❤️

Happy Birthday once again, my beautiful soul. 🎂🥳❤️ Waxaan kuu rajeynayaa nolol dheer, caafimaad, farxad, guul iyo wax kasta oo qalbigaagu doonayo. Ilaahay ha kaa yeelo qof mar walba farxad ku nool, hana kaa fogeeyo wax kasta oo qalbigaaga dhaawici kara. ❤️✨

I love and appreciate you more than words can explain. Happy Birthday, my special person. 🥹🎂❤️🫂

With so much love ♡ From: Mashkur Dahir 💙
